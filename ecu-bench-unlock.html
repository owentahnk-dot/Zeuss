What Is Bench Unlocking?

Bench unlocking is the process of unlocking your vehicle’s Engine Control Unit (ECU) by physically connecting to it outside the car — typically removed and worked on a test bench. This method allows deeper access than OBD, enabling full unlocks for tuning, cloning, or feature modification.

Common Applications

  • Preparing for Stage 1/2/3 Tuning
  • TCU/ECU Cloning or Replacement
  • Immobilizer & Component Matching
  • Advanced Diagnostics & Logging Access
  • Unlocking Factory Restrictions

ECU Bench Unlock for Post-June 2020 BMW & Toyota Supra

If you own a BMW or Toyota GR Supra manufactured after June 2020, you may have encountered limitations with traditional ECU tuning methods. These vehicles are equipped with Bosch MG1 or MD1 ECUs that are locked to prevent unauthorized access, making standard bench unlock procedures ineffective.

🚀 Introducing Femto ECU Unlock

The Femto ECU Unlock is a specialized service provided by Femto in Finland, designed to bypass the advanced encryption in post-June 2020 ECUs. This unlock is currently the only globally recognized method to enable OBD flash tuning on these models.

🔧 What We Offer

  • Secure and Reliable Unlocking: We facilitate the shipping of your ECU to Femto in Finland, where the unlocking process is performed. Once unlocked, the ECU is returned to you, ready for tuning.
  • Compatibility with Leading Tuning Platforms: After unlocking, your ECU will be compatible with popular tuning software such as Bootmod3 (BM3), MHD, and MG Flasher, allowing for custom ROMs, map switching, and other advanced tuning features.
  • Professional Handling: Our team ensures that your ECU is safely shipped, handled, and returned, minimizing downtime and ensuring a seamless experience.

⚠️ Important Considerations

  • Dealer Updates: Be aware that dealer updates can re-lock your ECU. If this occurs, the ECU will need to be sent back to Femto for re-unlocking, which may incur additional costs.
  • Turnaround Time: The entire process typically takes 5 to 10 business days, depending on shipping times and processing.
  • Shipping and Costs: While we assist with the shipping process, customers are responsible for the shipping costs to and from Femto.
